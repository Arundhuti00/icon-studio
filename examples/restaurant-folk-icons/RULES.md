# Restaurant Folk Icons — Rules v2.2 (locked)

v2 replaces v1; v2.1 adds translucent layering; v2.2 requires layers to follow the object's edges. v1 was illustrative spot art (19–31 shapes per icon, sparkles, shadows, tilt) and read as illustration, not icons.

## Rules
1. Flat colour shapes; no outlines around objects; no gradients.
2. 64 × 64 grid, 4 px padding, coordinates on a 0.5 grid.
3. **At most 12 shapes per icon.** Must read at 32 px, and in silhouette at 24 px.
4. Two tones per object part: base plus one shade. Light from upper-left, shade on the right.
5. At most 2 interior marks (one squiggle, a facet line, a few dots). Strokes are 2 px, round caps, never outlines.
6. Ornament: one simple row of almonds or scallops, only on the handi, chai glass and cloche.
7. Botanical only when it is part of the object (lime wedge, chilli stem). No added leaves, vines or flowers.
8. Translucent layering (from the reference): fill-opacity 0.6, 0.7 or 0.8, only on a shape that overlaps another, at most 3 per icon, and not on every icon. Used on the cocktail, cutting chai base, kulhad drip, wine glass, beer foam drip and rolling-pin dough edge.
9. No sparkles, no shadows, no rotation, no faces, no text.
10. Front or side view, upright. Same optical size across the set.
11. Palette (19 swatches, unchanged from v1): maroon/crimson, pink, tangerine/peach, mustard ochre, olive/chartreuse, cream highlight. Cream background on render; SVGs are transparent.
12. **Cohesive layers.** Every shade and inner layer starts and ends on the object's own edge or a natural seam (a notch, a rim, a fold), never at an arbitrary point inside the shape. No free-floating overlaps. **When unsure, use the fewest parts.**

## Do-not-copy list
- Menu reference: the hand and glass, the top-down citrus drink, the "menu / COCKTAILS" card, the tablecloth grid, the daisy, the drink's face.
- Crocodile reference: the crocodile, the wavy striped frame, the thistle flowers, the vine.
