# Travel Ink · Rules v2.3

Style taken from the bike illustration reference: thin dark ink line drawing, flat unoutlined colour blocks, chunky soft colour strokes, striped texture.

## Rules
1. 48 × 48 grid, 4 px padding (live area 40 × 40), coordinates on a 0.5 grid.
2. **Ink**: dark grey `#2E2E2E`, about 2 px, round ends. Ink draws the object and is always the largest share.
3. **Accents** *(v2.1)*: leaf green `#5DB85C` and tangerine `#FF8A2B` only, and **every icon uses both**. Yellow is removed.
4. **Accent block**: a flat colour shape with no outline, sitting under the ink. Ink may cross it.
5. **Accent stroke**: 4 px chunky round stroke for wheels, rings, straps and strings.
6. **No filled object bodies.** Every object is an ink outline. Solid ink fill only for small marks inside an outline (plane glyph, snap dot). *(v1.1: replaces the v1.0 "one solid ink fill" rule, which only the passport used.)*
7. **Stripes**: parallel 2 px ink lines, gap ≥ 5 px, on one part at most.
8. At most 10 shapes per icon. Must read at 24 px and in silhouette at 16 px.
9. Front or side view, upright, no rotation, no shading, gradients, shadows or text.
10. **Hand-drawn line** *(v2.0)*: every stroke swells and thins about ±24% along its length and drifts up to 0.25 px. Icons are drawn clean on the 0.5 grid, then a sketch script generates the hand-drawn outlines in `icons/` (rounded to 0.1).
11. Same optical size across the set (keylines: circle ∅40, portrait 32 × 40, landscape 40 × 32).

## Sizes *(v2.3)*
One drawing for every size: there is no separate small set. At 16 px the 48 px master renders at one-third scale, so the 2 px ink line is about ⅔ px and the 4 px accent strokes about 1⅓ px.
