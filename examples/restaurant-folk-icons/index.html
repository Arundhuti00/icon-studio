How every icon is built

  • ShapesFlat colour, no outlines, no gradients; 12 shapes at most.
  • TonesBase plus one shade, light from upper left.
  • MarksTwo interior marks at most, 2 px round strokes.
  • OrnamentOne almond or scallop row, only on the handi, chai glass and cloche.
  • BotanicalOnly when part of the object, like the lime or the chilli stem.
  • LayeringTranslucent fills at 60–80% only where shapes overlap; 3 per icon at most.
  • Cohesive layersShades start and end on the object's own edge or seam. When unsure, use the fewest parts.
  • RemovedNo sparkles, shadows, rotation, faces or text.
  • Size testReads at 32 px, and in silhouette at 24 px.
