How every icon is built

  • Grid64 × 64 with 4 px padding; geometry on a 0.5 grid.
  • PaletteBrown and blue shades only: six golden browns from espresso to latte, five blues from navy to ice.
  • GradientsEvery main surface runs clearly from light to deep; spheres lit from the upper left; trails and flames fade out.
  • Blended interiorsBands, land, craters and cores are blurred and clipped to the shape, so nothing inside has a hard seam.
  • Soft edges and glowEvery icon has softened edges; bright objects glow in their own colour.
  • GrainFine light and dark grain over every icon, kept inside the shape.
  • StrokesOnly for lines that are the object: 3 px or 5 px, round ends.
  • Budget10 shapes and 3 interior marks at most; reads at 24 px and in silhouette at 16 px.
  • CharactersFront view, upright, no text; only the alien, robot and space dog get simple eyes.
